# Changelog

What changed in each release of the mod, newest first. The first line under a version is what the launcher shows as the notes of that release.

## 0.1.0

Join a match in progress, Steam avatars for late joiners, and a fly camera.

- The host keeps the room open after the match starts and walks an invited friend into the running match.
- A friend who joins late sees the other balls and their movement, and waits at the scoreboard with everyone when they arrive between holes.
- The scoreboard shows a late joiner's Steam avatar instead of a plain ball.
- A free camera on F6 that only you see.
